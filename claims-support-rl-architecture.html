claims-support-rl

Training a small model to handle one device-insurance support turn, with GRPO.

Top zone
Runs once. Components are sampled in two steps — device first, then claims_last_12m within that device's claim limit — and each row is labelled with a gold route by rules checked in order.
Middle zone
One lap per task. GRPO compares the eight answers against their own mean and pushes the model toward the ones above it. Nothing here is written by hand — TRL supplies the trainer.
Bottom zone
Every rubric item carries check: code or check: judge. Code checks are exact and free; judge calls cross the network to the Mac. Eight answers times roughly eight judge items is 64 calls per task, which sets the training pace.
