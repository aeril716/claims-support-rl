# Phone deductibles (service fees)

A deductible is paid once per approved claim. It is non-refundable.

## Damage and malfunction claims

| Phone retail price | Repair | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| $150 – $199 | $10 | $29 |
| $200 – $249 | $19 | $39 |
| $250 – $399 | $29 | $49 |
| $400 – $699 | $39 | $99 |
| $700 or more | $49 | $129 |

Whether a claim is fulfilled by repair or replacement is decided by the plan, based on the phone model, the problem, and parts availability. The customer cannot choose replacement when a repair is available.

Cracked front screen and back glass repairs on eligible models: $29 flat.

## Loss and theft claims (phones only)
Deductible is tiered by device: $19 / $99 / $109 / $189 / $229 / $279. Most current flagship phones fall in the $229 or $279 tier. The exact tier for a model is shown when the claim is started.
