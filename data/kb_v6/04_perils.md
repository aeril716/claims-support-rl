# Perils (incident types)

Each claim must name exactly one primary peril.

## Covered perils
| Peril | Typical description | Coverage starts |
|---|---|---|
| Cracked screen | front or back glass cracked or shattered | day 31 |
| Drop / impact damage | body dented, buttons or ports damaged after a fall | day 31 |
| Liquid damage | spill, sink, rain, pool; device wet inside | day 31 |
| Power surge | device dead or charging port fried after electrical surge | day 31 |
| Battery failure | battery will not hold charge, swollen battery | day 31 |
| Mechanical/electrical failure | won't turn on, screen flickers, speaker dead, no drop or spill | after manufacturer warranty |
| Wear and tear | button sticks, hinge loose, port worn out from normal use | after manufacturer warranty |
| Dust / heat / humidity | failure from environmental exposure, not sudden immersion | after manufacturer warranty |
| Lost device | customer cannot find the device | day 31, phones only |
| Stolen device | device taken; police report may be requested | day 31, phones only |

## Not covered (claim will be denied)
- customer damaged the device on purpose
- scratches, scuffs, dents; device works normally
- damage happened before enrollment or during waiting period
- app, OS, virus, or account problems
- item not in the original box
- defect still covered by manufacturer
- lost photos, contacts, files
