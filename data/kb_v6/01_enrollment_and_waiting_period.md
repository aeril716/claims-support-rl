# Enrollment and waiting period

- A device can be enrolled within 30 days of purchase or activation. After 30 days it is no longer eligible.
- The plan and the device must be tied to the same customer account.
- Tech support starts the day of enrollment.
- Protection coverage (repairs and replacements) starts on day 31 after enrollment. A claim for an incident that happened before day 31 will be denied.
- Pre-existing damage is never covered, even after the waiting period.
- Registering the device (uploading the receipt) is optional but makes claims faster.
- Coverage continues month to month as long as the monthly fee is paid, up to 36 months per device.
