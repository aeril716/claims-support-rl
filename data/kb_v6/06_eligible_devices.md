# Eligible devices

| Category | Examples | ADH (drops/spills) | Loss/theft |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone | smartphones, feature phones | yes | yes |
| Laptop | laptops, Chromebooks, 2-in-1s | yes | no |
| Tablet | tablets, e-readers | yes | no |
| Watch | smartwatches, fitness wearables | yes | no |

Included with a laptop, tablet, or desktop: one associated mouse, keyboard, and external monitor per device. Not included: any other accessory bought separately.

Devices must be new or certified refurbished and purchased in the United States. Coverage is not available for use outside the U.S.
