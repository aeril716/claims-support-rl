# Claim limits

- Phones, loss/theft and damage combined: 3 claims in any rolling 12-month period.
- Laptops, tablets, watches: 2 claims in any rolling 12-month period per device.
- Maximum value per claim: $3,500. Anything above that is the customer's responsibility.
- Tech support calls are unlimited and do not count as claims.
- A denied claim does not count toward the limit.
- Once the limit is reached, the plan stays active (tech support continues) but no further repair or replacement claims are accepted until the oldest claim is more than 12 months old.
