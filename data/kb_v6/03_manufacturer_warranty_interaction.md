# Manufacturer warranty and the plan

- While the manufacturer's warranty is active (12 months (365 days) from enrollment), the manufacturer is responsible for defects and malfunctions. The plan will refer those claims to the manufacturer.
- The manufacturer does NOT cover accidental damage or power surge; the plan covers those from day 31 regardless of warranty status.
- After the manufacturer's warranty ends, the plan covers malfunctions and wear and tear.
