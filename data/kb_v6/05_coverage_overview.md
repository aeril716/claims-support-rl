# Coverage overview

- Service contract: covers accidental damage, malfunction, and wear and tear. Applies to phones, laptops, tablets, and watches.
- Insurance: covers loss and theft. Applies to phones only.
Both layers sit on top of the manufacturer's warranty; the plan does not replace or extend the warranty.

## Covered from day one (coverage start date)
- Accidental damage from handling (ADH): drops, spills, liquid, cracked screens
- Power surge failures
- Battery failure that prevents normal use
- 24/7 tech support

## Covered only after the manufacturer's warranty ends
- Mechanical or electrical malfunction from a defect in materials or workmanship
- Failures from normal wear and tear
- Failures from dust, heat, or humidity

## Phones only (insurance component)
- Loss
- Theft

## Not covered
- Intentional damage
- Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents, dings) when the device still works normally
- Pre-existing damage from before enrollment
- Accessories that did not come in the original box (cases, screen protectors, earbuds, chargers bought separately)
- Software problems, viruses, or operating system issues
- Loss of data, photos, or contacts
- Devices with a missing or altered serial number / IMEI
- Malfunctions that the manufacturer's warranty still covers
- Loss or theft of laptops, tablets, or watches (insurance component is phones only)
