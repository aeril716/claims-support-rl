# Laptop, tablet, and watch deductibles

Flat deductible per approved claim, regardless of the problem:

| Device | Deductible |
|---|---|
| Laptop | $129 |
| Tablet | $99 |
| Watch / wearable | $49 |

Repair vs replacement is decided by the plan. If the device cannot be repaired, the customer receives a replacement or a reimbursement up to the device's original purchase price.
