# Cancellation and billing

- Billed monthly to the customer's account. The fee varies by device category.
- Cancel any time. Within the first 30 days: full refund. After 30 days: prorated refund of the current month.
- The plan ends automatically if the monthly fee is unpaid for 10 days, if the device is no longer active on the account, or immediately if a claim is found to be fraudulent.
- An open claim must be completed or withdrawn before cancellation takes effect.
- Cancelling and re-enrolling later restarts the 30-day waiting period and requires the device to be undamaged.
