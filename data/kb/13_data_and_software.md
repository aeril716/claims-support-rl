# Data and software

- The plan never covers lost data. Photos, contacts, messages, and files on a repaired or replaced device are not recoverable through the plan.
- Customers should back up before sending a device for repair; repairs may wipe the device.
- Software problems (apps crashing, OS update failed, viruses, forgotten passcodes) are not claims. They are handled by tech support at no charge.
- If a software problem turns out to be caused by a hardware fault (e.g., a failing storage chip), it becomes a malfunction claim.
