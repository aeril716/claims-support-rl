# Loss and theft (phones only)

- Covered for phones from day 31. Not covered for laptops, tablets, or watches.
- Customer should immediately: suspend the line with the carrier, and enable lost mode / remote lock.
- File the claim within 90 days.
- A police report may be requested for theft claims. Loss claims need a description of when and where the phone was last seen.
- Deductible: tiered $19 to $279 depending on the model.
- Replacement ships as soon as next business day after approval.
- If the original phone is later found, it must be returned within 30 days or the customer keeps both and the deductible is not refunded.
- Counts toward the 3-claims-per-12-months limit.
