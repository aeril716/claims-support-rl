# How to file a claim

1. Start the claim online at the claims portal or by phone. Online takes about 10 minutes and is the fastest route.
2. Have ready: the plan order number, the device receipt or serial/IMEI, and a description of what happened and when.
3. Answer the questions about the incident. Most claims are approved within minutes; some are sent for review and answered within 1–2 business days.
4. Pay the deductible. The claim is not completed until the deductible is paid.
5. Receive the repair appointment, shipping label, or replacement device instructions.

## Deadlines
- Damage or malfunction: report within 60 days of the incident.
- Loss or theft: report within 90 days of the incident. Also suspend the phone line with the carrier immediately.

## Returning the damaged device
When a replacement is sent, the damaged device must be returned within 10 days using the prepaid label. If it is not returned, a non-returned equipment charge of up to $1,500 is billed.

## Documentation
The plan may ask for photos of the damage, a police report (theft), or proof of purchase. A claim without requested documents is put on hold, not denied.
