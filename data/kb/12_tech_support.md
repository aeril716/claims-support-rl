# Tech support

- Available 24/7 by phone or chat from the day of enrollment, no waiting period.
- Unlimited; does not count as a claim and has no fee.
- Covers: device setup, transferring contacts and photos, connecting to Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, app and account troubleshooting, performance tips, connecting to printers or TVs.
- Does not cover: physical repairs, data recovery from a broken device, or issues with third-party services' own accounts (e.g., resetting a bank app password).
- One-time setup session is included with every replacement phone.
