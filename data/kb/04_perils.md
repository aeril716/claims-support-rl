# Perils (incident types)

A peril is the reason a device needs service. Each claim must name exactly one primary peril.

## Covered perils
| Code | Peril | Typical description | Coverage starts |
|---|---|---|---|
| crack | Cracked screen | front or back glass cracked or shattered | day 31 |
| drop | Drop / impact damage | body dented, buttons or ports damaged after a fall | day 31 |
| liquid | Liquid damage | spill, sink, rain, pool; device wet inside | day 31 |
| surge | Power surge | device dead or charging port fried after electrical surge | day 31 |
| battery | Battery failure | battery will not hold charge, swollen battery | day 31 |
| malfunction | Mechanical/electrical failure | won't turn on, screen flickers, speaker dead, no drop or spill | after manufacturer warranty |
| wear | Wear and tear | button sticks, hinge loose, port worn out from normal use | after manufacturer warranty |
| environment | Dust / heat / humidity | failure from environmental exposure, not sudden immersion | after manufacturer warranty |
| loss | Lost device | customer cannot find the device | day 31, phones only |
| theft | Stolen device | device taken; police report may be requested | day 31, phones only |

## Not covered (claim will be denied)
| Code | Reason |
|---|---|
| intentional | customer damaged the device on purpose |
| cosmetic | scratches, scuffs, dents; device works normally |
| preexisting | damage happened before enrollment or during waiting period |
| software | app, OS, virus, or account problems |
| accessory | item not in the original box |
| warranty | defect still covered by manufacturer |
| data | lost photos, contacts, files |
