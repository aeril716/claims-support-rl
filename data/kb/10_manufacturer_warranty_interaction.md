# Manufacturer warranty and the plan

- While the manufacturer's warranty is active (usually 12 months), the manufacturer is responsible for defects and malfunctions. The plan will refer those claims to the manufacturer.
- The manufacturer does NOT cover accidental damage or power surge; the plan covers those from day 31 regardless of warranty status.
- After the manufacturer's warranty ends, the plan covers malfunctions and wear and tear.
- Example: a 6-month-old laptop that stops turning on with no drop or spill → manufacturer. Same laptop, 14 months old → plan, $129 deductible. Same laptop at 6 months after a coffee spill → plan, $129 deductible.
