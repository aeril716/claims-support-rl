# Common scenarios and correct outcomes

| Scenario | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Phone dropped, screen cracked, still works | crack claim, $29 screen repair if model eligible |
| Phone fell in pool, won't turn on | liquid claim, repair or replacement per price tier |
| Laptop 4 months old, keyboard stopped working, no damage | manufacturer warranty, plan refers customer |
| Laptop 4 months old, coffee spill | liquid claim; if approved, the laptop deductible is $129 |
| Tablet screen scratched, works fine | cosmetic damage, not covered by the plan |
| Watch lost at gym | loss is not covered for watches |
| Phone stolen from car | theft claim; a police report may be requested; tiered deductible |
| Phone enrolled 2 weeks ago, dropped yesterday | incident falls inside the waiting period, not covered |
| 4th phone claim this year | all 3 phone claims already used in the last 12 months; goes to a human agent, who reviews the earlier claims |
| Phone won't update iOS | software, route to tech support, no claim |
| Replacement phone arrived, old phone not returned after 3 weeks | non-return charge up to $1,500 |
| Customer wants replacement but repair is available | plan chooses repair; customer cannot override |
