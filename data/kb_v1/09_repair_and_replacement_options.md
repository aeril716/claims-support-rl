# Repair and replacement options

The plan decides repair vs replacement. The customer is then offered whichever options are available for that device and location.

## Repair
- In-store: appointment at an authorized repair location, same-day for cracked screens on eligible phones.
- In-home: a technician comes to the customer's address (select phones and areas).
- Mail-in: prepaid label, typically 3–5 business days round trip.
- Local repair reimbursement: customer pays a local shop and is reimbursed up to the approved amount.

## Replacement
- Advanced exchange: a replacement ships as soon as next business day; the old device is returned afterward.
- Reimbursement: an e-gift card for the original purchase price (used when no repair or replacement stock is available).

## Replacement device
- May be new or certified refurbished, same model or equivalent, same color when available.
- Parts used in repairs may be original or non-original manufacturer parts, which can void the remaining manufacturer warranty.
- Every repair and replacement carries its own 12-month limited warranty from the plan.
